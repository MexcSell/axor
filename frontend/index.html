    Avoid & caution

    Subnets where you can't profitably get in right now (avoid), or that are workable but hide a specific trap (caution). Every flag shows the measurement behind it.

    New subnets

    Subnets registered in the last 4 months, newest first. Early subnets can pay well before competition arrives, but carry more rule-change and deregistration risk.

    Unverified & high-risk signals

    These are rule-based warning signals from on-chain data and published identity, not verified facts and not a verdict of fraud. Each card shows the reasons it was flagged; the data refreshes with the chain. A subnet lands here when it draws emission while being anonymous, abandoned or for sale, or when its owner or a single closed-source hotkey captures almost all miner rewards. Check the reasons and do your own research.
