## Whiteboard

[Whiteboard](#whiteboard) lists your boards with thumbnails and a template gallery: blank, product launch, start/stop/continue, brainstorm, Kanban, flowchart, timeline, mind map, system design, SWOT and weekly plan. `#whiteboard/<id>` links to one board.

### On a board

- **Items:** sticky notes, text, shapes with text, connection lines that stay attached, pen, highlighter, frames, images, tables, charts, cards, documents, link cards and comment threads. Most items rotate (Shift for 15° steps).
- **Editing:** select and marquee, smart alignment guides, Alt-drag to copy, group, lock, align and distribute, layer order, copy and paste between boards, undo and redo, and a right-click menu. Press [[?]] for every shortcut.
- **Getting around:** Ctrl+wheel or pinch zooms, the wheel, Space-drag or the hand tool pans; the minimap and grid are in the zoom menu, and Ctrl+F finds.

### Together

Everyone on a board sees each other's cursors and selections, who's here (go to them or follow their view) and changes the moment they're saved. When two people edit at once, changes merge item by item. **Present** shows frames as slides.

### The AI assistant

The assistant writes sticky ideas, a flowchart from a description, a summary of the selection, or groups selected stickies into themed frames. It uses the agents' model and follows the agents access setting.

**Export** PNG (board or selection), SVG, a board file or a text outline for agents.

## My profile

[My profile](#profile) turns your AI engineer profile into a **resume**, a full **CV** and a personal **website**.

- **Profile:** name, headline, photo, location, summary, links, skills, experience, projects, education, publications and certifications. Everything saves as you type, and **profile strength** says what's missing.
- **Resume & CV:** two templates (*Modern* and *Classic*, which suits applicant tracking systems), a live preview, **Download PDF** and an HTML file.
- **Website:** a portfolio styled like a product site, in three themes and any accent color.
- **AI review** rewrites the headline, summary and role highlights, tailored to a pasted job post if you like, and lists missing keywords. It keeps your facts, and you tick which suggestions to apply.

### Publishing

**Publish** puts the site on AXoR at `/u/<address>` (with `/resume` and `/cv`), public and without sign-in. You can also **deploy to Netlify** with your own token, or **download a zip** for any static host. Public pages show your email and phone only if you turn that on.
