## Two tracks

[Study](#study) has two learning paths:

- **AI Engineer:** foundations, how LLMs work, building with LLM APIs, RAG, agents and tool use, evaluation, fine-tuning and open models, production, portfolio and interviews.
- **English C1+:** speaking fluency, pronunciation and intonation, advanced grammar, vocabulary, register, writing, listening, English at work and exams.

Set your level (CEFR for English), goal and hours a week per track. The path and your streak work for every signed-in user.

## The fast track

The **Roadmap** tab re-orders the path into nine steps that get you working at a high level soonest. Each step says what you'll be able to do, its topics, a build task, the hours and a checkpoint drill. Steps are marked done, *you are here*, next or later, with a date for each and a finish line at your pace.

- **Today's sprint** splits your daily time into learn, practise and review or build.
- **Test out** gives eight questions on a whole step and ticks it at 80%.
- **Plan it with a tutor** asks for a day-by-day plan.

A day counts toward your **streak** once you tick a topic or chat with a tutor.

## Practice

The **Practice** tab writes drills for your level, goal and topic: **Teach me a topic**, **Quiz me**, **Grammar workout**, **Concept flashcards** and **Vocabulary cards** (spaced repetition: Again, Hard, Good, Easy), **Coding practice** with a line-by-line review, **Correct my writing** with a word-level diff and a CEFR level, **Pronunciation drill**, **Dictation**, **Plan my week**, **Project idea**, and hands-free voice sessions: **Conversation**, **Debate**, **Job interview** and **System design interview**, each ending in a scored report.

> [!TIP]
> **Practise from your own material:** add Drive files or Source code and lessons, quizzes, flashcards and reviews are built from them. Files that look like secrets are skipped.

## The study team

**Create study team** makes six tutors: Ada (AI engineering mentor), Theo (AI engineering tutor), Hugo (interview coach), Clara (conversation partner), Grace (writing and grammar coach) and Leo (vocabulary and pronunciation coach). The chat icon on a topic opens its tutor with the topic as the focus. Tutors know your step and pace, read files you attach, tick topics once you've shown you know them, and remember your first language and recurring errors.

## The Lab

The **Lab** (AI Engineer track) runs entirely in your browser: Python on Pyodide in a Web Worker, so nothing you run reaches the server, and code there can't reach AXoR's API.

| Tab | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Terminal** | A shell with files, pipes and redirects, `python`, `pip install`, `pytest`, `make` and `sqlite3` |
| **Python** | An editor with your files, `input()`, and matplotlib output |
| **SQL** | A sample LLM-app database (users, model calls, eval runs) or any `.db` in your home folder |
| **Prompts** | A system prompt and turns sent to one model or two side by side, as text or JSON matching your schema |

**Get a task** asks the tutor for a hands-on task with tests; **Check** runs them in the browser. **Projects** are onboarding repositories (minitok, gateway, evalkit): read the onboarding doc, run `make test`, then work through tickets from a good first issue to the 0.1.0 release, with **Run CI** checking each one.

Your Lab files stay in this browser, per account; `reset` empties them.
