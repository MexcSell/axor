The [Command Center](#command) (sidebar → Command → Overview) is where you land after signing in. Every number on it comes from AXoR's own systems: the [Company](#help/company), [Workforce](#help/workforce), [Competition teams](#help/mining), My subnets, the chain and the services you've set up. A number AXoR can't read is left out rather than guessed.

## The four cards at the top

- **Agents**: every agent you can see across AXoR (the same count as [Agent Operations](#help/agent-operations)), how many are working, and a bar of how they split by system.
- **Active**: runs happening right now in any system. The line under it is tasks done per day over the last 7 days.
- **Competitions**: the subnet competitions you work on, from your Competition teams and the miners My subnets tracks, with how many of the teams' tasks are done.
- **Approvals**: requests waiting for your decision, split by the system that asked.

Each card opens the page behind it.

## The cards below

- **Agent activity**: tasks done per day, or tokens used per day, over the last 7 days. Switch with **Tasks** and **Tokens**; each has its own scale.
- **Bittensor network**: the subnet of the first miner My subnets tracks: your rank, what the subnet pays its miners per day, how many miners earn, and the median miner's earnings. Without a tracked miner it shows the chain: subnets, the TAO price and the latest block.
- **System health**: the chain and the services that are set up (agents, workspace services, infrastructure). A service that isn't set up isn't counted against the health.
- **Pending approvals**: the three most urgent requests. **Review** opens each on its own page, with its evidence, where you approve or reject it. Nothing is approved from the Command Center: production actions always wait for you on the page that owns them. All of them are in the [Approval Center](#approvals).
- **Recent activity**: the latest things your agents did, topped up with notifications.
- **Quick actions**: shortcuts to create an agent, start a project, import a competition, run the Workflow, see evaluations or open your workspace. You only see the ones your account can use.
- **AI Assistant**: a summary of right now, a few questions to start with, and a box to ask your own. The answer opens in the Assist panel, in your chat with the Assistant, which reads live AXoR data. It needs agents and a model.

## The tabs at the bottom

- **Active missions**: every run in progress, with its agent, status, where it runs and when it started.
- **Running agents**: agents per system and how many are working, and your Competition teams' progress.
- **System events**: your latest notifications.
- **Performance**: your miners' ranks and τ per day, model use by source, and team budgets for today.

## Around every page

- The sidebar's seven sections are described in [Welcome to AXoR](#help/welcome). **Performance lab**, **Experiments**, **Miner performance**, **Rewards & costs** and **Live operations** open those screens in My subnets.
- The line at the foot of the sidebar and the status bar along the bottom show the same health, plus your agents, projects and pending approvals. Administrators also see the server's CPU, memory and disk in the status bar; the CPU and MEM meter in the top bar turns those on and off.
