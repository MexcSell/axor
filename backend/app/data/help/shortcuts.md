## The page guide

The **map** button in the top bar (on a phone, **Page guide** in the menu) is a guide to the page you're on.

- **Tour this page** walks through what the page shows right now: its parts, tabs and main buttons, one at a time. [[→]] and [[←]] move between steps, [[Esc]] ends the tour. The first time you open a page, a card offers its tour, once per page.
- **Suggested values:** click into a field and AXoR suggests values under it, from live chain data (like today's registration costs on Recommendations), your account (your email, your workspace subnets) or the form's own example, with the values you entered there before. A click fills the field in; nothing is saved or sent until you submit. [[Alt]]+[[↓]] moves into the suggestions, [[Esc]] hides them.
- Switch suggestions and tour offers off in the same menu. **Offer every tour again** brings back the first-visit cards, and **Forget values I entered** clears what the guide remembers.

The guide's settings and remembered values stay in the browser, for your account only. It never remembers or suggests passwords, keys, tokens, wallet addresses or amounts to send or stake, and it doesn't remember what you type in long text boxes.

## Writing help

Text boxes on every page have an AI writing assistant. Type a few words and pause: it checks spelling, grammar and punctuation, and the small button in the box's corner shows how many fixes it has (a tick when there's nothing to fix).

- **Automatic fixes:** when you leave the box, small mistakes are fixed for you and a note beside it offers **Undo**. Bigger changes (more than a few words, or lines added or dropped) are never applied without a click.
- **Better wording:** click the button (or press [[Alt]]+[[Shift]]+[[W]] in the box) for the corrections, with what changed and why, and two or three better versions of what you wrote. **Use this** puts one in the box.
- **Your idea:** type what you're after in the panel's box, like *friendlier*, *shorter* or *mention my RTX 4090*, and **Reword** writes new versions that follow it. It keeps your facts and adds none.
- The gear in the panel turns off checking as you type or the automatic fixes, and **Don't help in this field** hides it in one box. [[Esc]] closes the panel.

It needs agents (an administrator turns them on and sets up a model) and spends the same credits; its use shows as *Writing help* in your token usage. Password, key, seed, address, search and code boxes are never checked, nor anything that looks like a seed phrase, a key or code. Your text goes to the model only while you write in a box that's checked.

## Links you can share

Every page has its own address, so you can bookmark or send one.

| Link | Opens |
|---|---|
| `#sn64` | Subnet 64's details |
| `#recommend`, `#caution`, `#new`, `#scam`, `#tech` | That page |
| `#workspace`, `#study`, `#wallet`, `#drive`, `#notifications` | That page (signed in) |
| `#agents/<id>` | An agent's chat |
| `#whiteboard/<id>` | One whiteboard |
| `#help/<doc>` and `#help/<doc>/<section>` | A help page, or a section of it (hover a heading and click **#** to copy its link) |

## Settings that follow you

Your **theme** (light, dark or system), **accent color**, **τ / $** unit and **recommendation profile** are saved to your account and come with you to every device. The sidebar's collapsed state and folded groups, the Explorer's filters and columns, and similar view choices stay in the browser you set them in.

## Small things worth knowing

- The **eye** button on Wallets hides your balances on screen, for screen sharing.
- Long agent answers fold; the **T** button in any chat sets text size, spacing and layout.
- Subnets named in agent answers (like SN19) are links to their details.
- On a phone, add AXoR to your home screen for push notifications and a full-screen app.
